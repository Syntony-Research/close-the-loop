What happens in hosted AI mode? +

When you run a live round, your synthetic message and campaign history are sent through the arena’s hosted service to the provider named on your selected target. Credentials stay on the server. No model weights are downloaded to your browser.

Practice runs entirely in this sandbox using the explicit action JSON. Live targets become selectable only when the service reports them available. A failed live request never silently becomes a practice result. Use synthetic content only; do not enter sensitive information.
